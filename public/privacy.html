Reach

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 23, 2026

Reach (reach-outs.app) helps you keep in touch with the people you care about. This page explains what Reach stores, why, and how to remove it.

What Reach stores

  • Your account: your email address, time zone, and which day you want the weekly email.
  • What you enter: contacts (name, relationship, how often to reach out, birthday, phone, email, notes, and an optional reminder line), groups, and the conversations you log.
  • Reminder history: when a reminder email was sent, so you do not get the same one twice.
  • Connections you turn on: an encrypted Google refresh token if you connect Gmail, and hashed access tokens if you connect an AI assistant.

Gmail

Connecting Gmail is optional. Reach asks for the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.metadata permission, which gives access to message headers and labels only. Google does not let this permission read message bodies or attachments.

When you open the Gmail review page, Reach reads the From, To, Cc, Date, and List-Unsubscribe headers of your 200 most recent inbox messages and 200 most recent sent messages. It uses them to suggest people you actually write back and forth with, and to skip mailing lists. These headers are passed to your browser to build the list. Reach does not save them. A person becomes a contact only when you choose Accept. That saves their name and email address, marked as coming from Gmail.

Reach stores the Google refresh token encrypted. It is never sent to your browser. Disconnect in Settings deletes it. You can also remove Reach at any time from your Google Account permissions.

Limited Use

Reach's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular, Reach:

  • uses Gmail data only to suggest contacts to you inside Reach;
  • does not sell it, or use it for advertising, credit decisions, or lending;
  • does not use it to train general artificial intelligence or machine learning models;
  • does not let any person read it, except with your permission, when needed for security or to follow the law.

AI assistants

If you connect an assistant such as Claude, it can find, read, create, and update your contacts and log conversations, only after you sign in and choose Allow. It cannot delete anything, and it has no access to Gmail. You can revoke access in Settings at any time.

Services that handle your data

  • Supabase: hosts the database, sign-in and sign-in emails, and server functions.
  • Vercel: hosts the website.
  • Resend: sends reminder emails to your address.
  • Google: provides Gmail access, only if you connect it.

Reach does not sell your data, show ads, or use tracking cookies.

Security

Traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. Database rules let each account read only its own rows. The Google refresh token is encrypted, and assistant tokens are stored only as hashes.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it. You can delete contacts in the app, and disconnect Gmail or an assistant in Settings. To delete your whole account and everything in it, email r0bberve3r@gmail.com from your account address. It will be removed within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change.
